☙ The Formula, In Plain Speech ❧

Cash is certainty

Cash removes the lender, the appraisal and most of the ways a deal dies in week three. The Crucible pays it the highest certainty premium — because sellers who have buried a cash-less deal already know why.

Earnest money is a tell

A buyer with real money down has something to lose by walking. One percent is a handshake. Five percent is a commitment.

Time is a line item

Every extra week to closing is carrying cost out of your pocket. A slower, higher offer can quietly cost more than a faster, lower one earns.

Contingencies are doors

Every contingency kept is a door the buyer can walk out of. Waived doors do not make a deal certain — they make escape more expensive.
